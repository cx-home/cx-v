=== test: 050-l002-mixed-long-short-type-form
level: core
tags: lint l002
# Document uses both :int and :i — minority form (whichever has
# fewer uses) is flagged. Tie goes to flagging short form. Two
# long-form (:int / :string) and one short-form (:i) → flag the
# short.
--- in_cx
[count :int 42]
[name :string alice]
[short :i 7]
--- expected_check_count
1
--- expected_check
CX-L002
--- expected_severity
info
